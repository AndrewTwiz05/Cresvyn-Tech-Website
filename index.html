FLAGSHIP PRODUCT · PILOT STAGE

NeoVoice™

Because every voice deserves to be heard.

Our India-developed electrolarynx is designed to support speech communication for people who have undergone a laryngectomy. Built with accessibility, ease of use, and local support in mind.

₹Designed for affordabilityTarget pricing substantially below imported alternatives
↔Simple, intuitive controlsPush-button operation with pitch and volume adjustment
ϟMade for daily useUSB-C rechargeable design

NeoVoice is in pilot-stage development. Regulatory certification is in progress; commercial availability is not yet announced.
